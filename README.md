# E-commerce-Business-Performance-Analysis
## Project Overview:
This project is an interactive E-commerce Business Intelligence Dashboard developed in Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and Marketing effectiveness. 
The Dashboard helps identify key business insights such as top-performing products, revenue trends, customer retention patterns, regional performance, and compaign efficiency. it is designed to support data-driven decision making for business growth and operational optimization.

## Key Business Objective:
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify high performing products and category
- Understand customer purchesing behavior
- Detect revenue leakage through cancellations and returns
- Provide actionable business recommendations

## Dashboard Pages
1- Executive Summery 
2- Sales & Product Analysis
3- Customer Analysis
4- Marketing Performance Analysis

## Tools & Technologies Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel

## Key Insights:
- Revenue concentration in top-performing products and regions
- Returning customers contribute significant business value
- High discounts impact profitability in some categories
- Certain campaigns generate low returns despite high ad spend
- Mobile users drive majority of customer transactions

This project demonstrates business-focused analytics, storytelling through data visualization, and decision-making insights commonly used in real-world e-commerce businesses.
